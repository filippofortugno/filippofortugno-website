Strategic Asset Valuation

Valuation Market Sizing Financial Modelling

Led the valuation workstream for a strategic asset project worth approximately €50M, building financial scenario models under multiple assumptions and sizing the addressable market to stress-test the business case.

Implementation details
Overview

Nexi needed to decide whether to sell or revamp the asset, which required a deep analysis to understand the cause of its decline and its actual state. The decision required significant Capex investment, but Nexi lacked a rigorous valuation of the underlying ~€50M strategic asset. I was asked to independently assess the asset's value, size the addressable market, and outline possible scenarios to inform stakeholders on the investment decision.

What I Did

Built a financial scenario model with three scenarios under multiple assumptions, ran sensitivity analysis on the key variables, and cross-checked market sizing against comparable transactions. I also built the deck used to present the findings to key stakeholders.

Result

The valuation directly informed top management's approval of the Capex-backed campaign to revamp the asset, giving decision-makers a data-backed view of the opportunity.

Customer Repricing Initiative

Market Analysis Customer Base Assessment Pricing Strategy

Ran market analyses and customer-base assessments across the client portfolio to identify where pricing was misaligned with value delivered, then built the case for a repricing initiative.

Implementation details
Overview

Parts of Nexi's client portfolio were priced below the value being delivered, leaving revenue on the table and creating friction with both new and existing clients. I was asked to identify where pricing was misaligned across the portfolio and build the business case for a repricing initiative.

What I Did

Ran market analyses and customer-base assessments, segmented clients by value delivered versus price paid, and built a Power BI dashboard to automate the process. Once the target segments were identified, I led the repricing initiative end-to-end, from analysis through implementation, managing the communication with both banks' account managers and the clients impacted by the change.

Result

About €450K in additional annual revenue generated from the initiative once approved and rolled out, restoring the Group's margin on clients that had previously been generating negative or below-threshold profitability.
